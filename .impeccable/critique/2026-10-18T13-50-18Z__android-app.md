Specificity verdict: identity lives in the character artwork; the layout is a stock Material column of plain text lines.

Overall: the characters are the best idea, but the screen does not act on it. The answer to "what do I wear" is the 64dp character, while all numbers are small and equal in weight. Biggest opportunity: the observed-plus-forecast timeline that the web has and Android lacks.

What's working
- Character artwork carries the headline message.
- 4-tab Material bar, gear and location switcher feel native.
- Freshness line (Updated / Updating / offline) makes data age honest.

Priority issues
1. [P1] Overview lacks the observed+forecast timeline, Now marker, rain/wind rows, 24h/3d/7d switch (shape).
2. [P1] Today card has no hierarchy; current temperature is 16sp; no condition word (typeset, layout).
3. [P1] Weather conveyed by images with null content descriptions; clickable notice text has no role or 48dp target (harden).
4. [P2] Theme defines ~9 color roles; nav pill/top bar/refresh indicator likely fall back to default lavender; hand-picked text sizes (typeset, polish).
5. [P2] No large-screen/foldable layout; no navigation rail (adapt).

Persona red flags: glance user, TalkBack user, tablet/foldable user.
Minor: bare hour labels "15"; weekly strip first "Mon" ambiguous; weekly icons never night; freshness line above warnings; pull-to-refresh vs horizontal rows.
